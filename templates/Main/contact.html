Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about RentEase

How do I get started with RentEase?

Getting started is easy! Sign up for a free trial on our website and you'll be guided through the setup process. Our onboarding team is also available to help you get everything configured.

What types of properties does RentEase support?

RentEase works with all types of rental properties including single-family homes, multi-unit apartments, vacation rentals, student housing, and commercial properties.

Is my data secure with RentEase?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and follow industry best practices to ensure your data is always protected. We're also GDPR compliant and never share your data with third parties.

Can I integrate RentEase with other software?

Yes! RentEase integrates with popular accounting software, payment processors, and other property management tools. Check our integrations page for the full list.

What support options are available?

We offer 24/7 email support, live chat during business hours, and phone support for premium customers. Our extensive knowledge base also contains helpful articles and video tutorials.

Do you offer training for new users?

Yes, we provide comprehensive onboarding sessions, weekly webinars, and personalized training for teams. All customers also get access to our video tutorial library.
